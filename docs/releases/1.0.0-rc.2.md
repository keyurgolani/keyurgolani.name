# 1.0.0-rc.2: known issues

These are the issues known in release candidate 1.0.0-rc.2. Each one under "Listed in the quality records" is in the release's quality records (`quality/release/records.json`), has an owner, and is planned for a later candidate. The issues under "Tracked separately" have no record but are known, owned and planned too. Version 1.0.0 ships only once every one of them is resolved and the records list is empty. What changed since 1.0.0-rc.1 is listed at the end.

What was checked: each composition was checked only with the default design language, color scheme and typography (Platform Neutral, Warm Parchment and Humanist UI), in every display mode (light, dark, system and high contrast). Studio, Demo Studio and the sign-in page follow the device's light or dark setting rather than a display mode, and were checked in light only. The other design languages, color schemes and typographies weren't checked for this release, except where an item below says so. Nor were the pages a portfolio shows when its site is unavailable or when it falls back to its last good version; the checks can't produce those yet. The automated checks ran in Chromium only, not in Firefox or Safari.

## Listed in the quality records

- **Chapters:** on a portfolio with missing pictures, on phones, the title page's line with the tagline, location and pronouns is low in contrast against its background.
- **Editorial Evidence:** on a project's page, two of the back links' focus rings are too thin or too faint to see clearly, also on phones. At 1440 px, one link in the page's navigation has the same problem in the high-contrast display mode, and with increased contrast or forced colors in every display mode. With text at 200%, the project's web address in its fact sheet doesn't wrap, so the page scrolls sideways at 1024 px wide.
- **Flux:**
  - With text at 200%, a project page's case sheet is laid out wider than the window at 1024 px, so its facts, headings and story run off the right edge and are cut.
  - On a narrow phone (320 px), on a project's page, a missing picture's "Image unavailable" label is cut off inside its frame.
  - On phones, the tab bar at the bottom shortens the owner's name with an ellipsis when it doesn't fit, so part of the name can't be read; on a narrow phone (320 px) even a 12-character name is cut.
  - On phones, two expandable sections' focus rings on the home page show only in part, one of them only with increased contrast, and with forced colors so does one link's on a project's page.
- **Horizontal Cinema:**
  - Printing: some timeline labels and dates, chapter numbers and track lengths print too faint to read.
  - On phones, the focus ring on the expandable section in some scenes can be hard to see.
  - On phones with increased contrast, in the light display mode or the system default, one control's focus ring shows only in part.
  - On a portfolio with missing pictures, in the chapter navigation at 1024 and 1440 px, hovering a link shows more than focusing it from the keyboard does.
  - In the high-contrast display mode with JavaScript off at 1024 px, one scene's "Next:" link is smaller than the minimum target size.
- **Kinetic Cosmos:** with reduced transparency at 1024 px, the index panel stays see-through in the light display mode and the system default, and on a portfolio with missing pictures in the dark display mode instead. On phones, on a portfolio with missing pictures, the tagline below the site name is drawn over the lower half of the site name's link, so our automated keyboard check counts the link as partly covered when it has focus. A tap on the tagline still reaches the link.
- **Obsidian Atelier:**
  - On phones, on a project's page, the skip link is hidden behind the site name when it gets focus.
  - On a project's page, the installation section's first link shows no visible focus ring in the light display mode or the system default.
  - With text at 200%, a work's wall label at 1024 px is too narrow for its longer words and is cut at the wall's edge.
  - On phones, on a portfolio with missing pictures: with increased contrast in the dark and high-contrast display modes, one header link's focus ring shows only in part, and with reduced transparency the first line of the entrance plaque is low in contrast.
- **Raw Grid:** on a project's page, the second and third links of its in-page navigation show only part of their focus ring.
- **Singular:** on a project's page, the project picture's caption can't be read against what's behind it.
- **Terra Field:** on a project's page, the link to the next project shows only part of its focus ring.
- **Swiss Signal:** on a phone, the contact email's underline runs into the bottom of its letters, which makes them hard to read. It shows in the dark and high-contrast display modes, and with increased contrast in every display mode.
- **Spatial Bento:** with text at 200%, the home page scrolls sideways at 1024 px wide in the light, dark and system display modes, with every kind of content, and in the system display mode of the 11 other design languages too, because the field's canvas keeps its width when its column shrinks.
- **Demo Studio:** with text at 200%, the "Demo sandbox. Only you can see these changes." line doesn't wrap and runs past its column at 1024 px.

## Tracked separately

- **Text at 200% on phones:** the page scrolls sideways at 390 and 320 px wide in Editorial Evidence, Obsidian Atelier, Terra Field, Swiss Signal and Schematic, and at 320 px in Mission Control and Spatial Bento. This release's quality checks run 200% text only at 1024 px.
- **Printing:** a printed page can include the visitor's Appearance controls (the color scheme menu, the display mode choices and "Reset to site default").
- **Aurora Glass:**
  - On phones, if the About text holds a very long word or web address, it runs past its card and is cut off at the page's edge.
  - In the Glassmorphism design language on phones, a stat tile's numbers read just under the contrast minimum, and one expandable section shows only part of its focus ring.
- **Editorial Evidence:** with increased contrast at 1440 px, in four design languages beyond the default (Carbon Inspired, International Grid, Neobrutalist and Soft Organic), coming back to the top of the page from a chapter leaves that chapter marked in the contents menu, with its section links still open under it. Our automated keyboard check found it: the links it had listed for that chapter closed before Tab reached them.
- **Horizontal Cinema:**
  - In right-to-left portfolios, "Next:" can appear after the next scene's name instead of before it, and the scene counter can show the scene's name and number in the wrong order (for example "1 of 12 ,Opening").
  - Scrolling the strip sideways while the window is being resized can lose the reader's place, and a sideways swipe made before a step to the next scene settles may do the same.
  - Changing the text size while reading the credits or the foot of the page, below the strip, can throw the page back up to the end of the strip.
  - In the List view, the owner's name at the top, a link home, is marked as the current page, so screen readers announce it as the page you're on.
- **Kinetic Cosmos:** with text at 200%, a project page's title can break in the middle of a word at 1024 px wide.
- **Raw Grid:** in the high-contrast display mode, and with increased contrast or forced colors, the ticker across the top of the page holds still, but its "Pause motion" button still shows.
- **Studio:**
  - If the script that starts Studio fails while it's starting up (not a slow or missing download, which Studio already handles), Studio stays on its loading view and no "couldn't load" message appears. Reloading the page is the way out.
  - With JavaScript off, Sign out doesn't work. And when Studio can't load or one of its pages fails, the "Studio couldn't load." message offers only Reload Studio, with no way to sign out from it.
- **Demo Studio, measurement:** in this release, how long the Compare grid keeps the page too busy to respond while the pointer moves across its previews (its total blocking time) is measured and reported but not held to a limit, because on the same code it read anywhere from 1 to 444 ms. A later candidate brings back a fixed limit for it.

## Changed since 1.0.0-rc.1

- **Dependencies:** zod moves from 3.25.76 to 4.6.6.
- **Checks:** every CI run on main now runs the release's production dependency scan, so a new High or Critical finding in what the app ships shows up before a release candidate is tagged.
- **Raw Grid:** in the high-contrast display mode, and with increased contrast or forced colors, the ticker across the top of the page no longer moves: it holds still and shows each of its items once, wrapping onto more lines where needed.
- **Studio:** after too many sign-in attempts, the countdown until you can try again now follows the clock, so it no longer falls behind when its tab is in the background. On the Preview page, the controls are disabled until the page is ready; before, a click or change made too early did nothing.
