# 1.0.0-rc.3: known issues

These are the issues known in release candidate 1.0.0-rc.3. Its quality records list (`quality/release/records.json`) is empty. Every issue 1.0.0-rc.2 listed there is resolved, and this candidate passes its quality checks with nothing listed. Every issue 1.0.0-rc.2 tracked separately is resolved too. The one issue below has no record. It's known and owned, and it's planned for a release after 1.0.0. What changed since 1.0.0-rc.2 is listed at the end.

What was checked: every public composition in each color scheme and display mode its default design language and typography accept, and every shipping design language, color scheme and typography paired with each of the others at least once across the compositions, on every page, state and surface. That's everything a visitor or the owner can choose. Studio, Demo Studio and the sign-in page follow the device's light or dark setting rather than a display mode, and were checked in light only. The quality checks ran text at 200% at 1024 px wide only. On phones, at 320 and 390 px, it's held by the tests of the nine compositions fixed for it. The pages a portfolio shows when its site is unavailable or when it falls back to its last good version weren't checked; the checks can't produce those yet. The automated checks ran in Chromium only, not in Firefox or Safari.

## Listed in the quality records

None.

## Tracked separately

- **Flux:** on a narrow phone (320 px), a missing picture's "Image unavailable" label can break inside the word "unavailable". The whole label still shows.

## Changed since 1.0.0-rc.2

- **Fixed:** the issues in 1.0.0-rc.2's quality records. Two of its records, Kinetic Cosmos's index panel and Obsidian Atelier's plaque line, were the checks misreading the page; the checks read them right now.
- **Fixed from 1.0.0-rc.2's tracked list:**
  - **Text at 200% on phones:** at 320 and 390 px wide, pages no longer scroll sideways in Chapters, Editorial Evidence, Horizontal Cinema, Mission Control, Obsidian Atelier, Schematic, Spatial Bento, Swiss Signal and Terra Field, with their folds and menus open or shut. Long words and addresses break inside their boxes, and grids, fact sheets, tables and tiles narrow to fit or take one column.
  - **Printing:** a printed page leaves out the visitor's Appearance controls.
  - **Aurora Glass:** a long word or web address in the About text breaks inside its card. In the Glassmorphism design language on phones, the stat tiles' numbers read above the contrast minimum.
  - **Horizontal Cinema:** in right-to-left portfolios, "Next:" and the scene counter read in the right order. The strip keeps the reader's place through a resize and through a sideways swipe made while a step settles, and a reader below the strip keeps their place when the text size changes. In the List view, the owner's name is no longer marked as the current page.
  - **Kinetic Cosmos:** with text at 200%, a project page's title keeps each word on one line.
  - **Raw Grid:** where the ticker holds still, as in the high-contrast display mode, its "Pause motion" button no longer shows.
  - Also fixed: Editorial Evidence's contents menu leaving a chapter marked back at the top of the page; Aurora Glass's expandable section in Glassmorphism on phones; Studio staying on its loading view when its script fails to start; and Sign out, which now works without JavaScript and from the "Studio couldn't load." message.
- **Other fixes:**
  - **Aurora Glass:** with JavaScript off, the page keeps no room for a Pause button it can't show.
  - **Editorial Evidence:** tabbing into the chapter index scrolls the page to the entry that has focus.
  - **Mission Control:** a readout's figure too long for its square is set small enough to fit it. With text at 200%, the Appearance panel and a work's long credit stay inside the window when they're open.
  - **Schematic:** in Paste-Up, the focus ring on a cover note's link to its figure clears the link's letters.
  - **Shell:** under forced colors, the key and value lines sit far enough apart that a focus ring doesn't cover them.
  - **Spatial Bento:** a phone's dock too narrow for its three labels keeps its Menu, which holds the works and Contact.
- **Fixes from the wider checks:** focus rings that showed only in part, links and buttons smaller than 24 px, colors that didn't hold under forced colors, and text that ran past its box at 200%, across every composition.
- **Color schemes:** scrollbars, a form field's placeholder and autofill, a dialog's backdrop and a tap's highlight take the color scheme. Studio draws its scrollbars and controls in its own scheme.
- **Typography:** Book Serif, Field Serif and Paste-Up's Archivo no longer move the page when they arrive late.
- **Skip link:** it shows above every composition's header when it has focus, and its text reads on its accent.
- **Dependencies:** Next.js moves from 16.4.1 to 16.4.5, a patch release with seven security fixes.
- **Checks:** the release's quality checks now cover every design language, color scheme and typography a visitor or the owner can choose, not only each composition's defaults. Every composition's screenshots are compared on every CI run; Flux, Kinetic Cosmos, Obsidian Atelier, Singular and Spatial Bento had none before. Every page's blocking time is held to its limit again, Demo Studio's Compare grid, Mission Control and Raw Grid included. The screenshot checks' render comparison starts again on a new page when its page goes away partway through, and their boundary capture reads the page in one step.
